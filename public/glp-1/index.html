What is GLP-1?

Understanding GLP-1 and Weight Management

GLP-1 is a naturally occurring hormone involved in appetite, digestion, and blood sugar regulation.

GLP-1-based approaches have become widely discussed in weight management because of their role in appetite signaling and how the body regulates food intake.

The right approach varies from person to person, which is why understanding your options is an important first step.

A role in appetite signaling

GLP-1 helps communicate feelings of fullness between the digestive system and brain.

A role in digestion

GLP-1 influences how quickly food moves through the digestive system.

A role in blood sugar

GLP-1 is involved in the body’s response to blood sugar after meals.

Part of a bigger picture

Nutrition, activity, sleep, and overall health all play a role in sustainable weight management.
